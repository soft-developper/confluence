# Changelog

## 28 September 2026

- **Relay routes are live.** Switch between **Confluence** and **Relay** on the Bridge and Swap pages to bridge and swap across Relay's chains and tokens. Every Relay quote is checked against Relay's published contracts before your wallet is asked to sign.
- Relay routes appear in your Profile history, with statuses that update on their own.
- The Privacy Policy and Terms of Use now cover Relay routes.
- The Terms now state the bridge fee over 1,000 USDC precisely: 0.10% of the whole amount.
- **These docs launched** at docs.confluencebuild.xyz.

## 27 September 2026

- **Confluence is live on mainnet**, bridging native USDC with Circle's CCTP across the chains where Circle's Forwarding Service is supported.
- Privacy Policy and Terms of Use published.
