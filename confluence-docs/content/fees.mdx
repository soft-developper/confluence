# Fees

Every fee is shown on the quote before you sign, and nothing is charged until you confirm in your wallet. This page explains each one.

## Bridge (CCTP)

| Bridge amount | Confluence platform fee |
| --- | --- |
| Up to and including 1,000 USDC | **0.30 USDC** |
| Over 1,000 USDC | **0.10% of the whole amount** |

The platform fee is **added on top** of the amount you bridge. The review screen shows the bridge amount, the platform fee, and the **total debit**: what actually leaves your wallet.

### Worked examples

| You bridge | Platform fee | Total debit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 50 USDC | 0.30 | 50.30 USDC |
| 1,000 USDC | 0.30 | 1,000.30 USDC |
| 1,000.01 USDC | 1.00001 | 1,001.01001 USDC |
| 5,000 USDC | 5.00 | 5,005.00 USDC |

<Callout type="info">
  The fee steps up at 1,000 USDC: at exactly 1,000 it's 0.30, just above it's about 1.00. If you're close to the line, sending 1,000 or less
  keeps the flat fee.
</Callout>

Percentage fees are rounded up to the smallest USDC unit (0.000001).

### Costs from Circle and the network

These are shown separately on the quote and aren't Confluence fees:

- **CCTP fee (Fast only):** Circle's fee for Fast Transfer. Standard transfers have no CCTP fee.
- **Forwarding fee (when Forwarding is on):** Circle's fee for delivering the mint on the destination chain. It's taken from the amount that arrives, so the quote's **expected receive** already accounts for it.
- **Network gas:** paid by your wallet on the source chain for the approval and the burn, and on the destination chain if you complete a mint yourself. On Arc, gas is paid in USDC.

## Swap

Confluence's swap fee follows the same rule, applied to the token the fee is taken in (shown on the quote):

- **Stablecoins** (USDC, EURC, USDT): 0.30 for amounts up to 1,000, and 0.10% of the whole amount above that.
- **Any other token:** 0.10% of the amount.

Circle's App Kit route and the network's gas are shown on the quote as well.

## Relay routes

- **Confluence fee:** a percentage of the value you send, shown on every Relay quote (0.10% by default). The rate on your quote is the one that applies.
- **Relay's own costs**, each shown on the quote:
  - **Execution cost:** gas and delivery on the route.
  - **Swap cost:** the cost of any swap within the route.
  - **Relay platform fee:** set by Relay and depending on the route. See Relay's [fee documentation](https://docs.relay.link/references/api/api_core_concepts/fees) for details.

<Callout type="warning">
  Always check the **total debit** or **you pay** line before confirming. Blockchain transactions can't be reversed, and that includes fees.
</Callout>
