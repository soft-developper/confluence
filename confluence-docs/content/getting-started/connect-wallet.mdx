# Connect your wallet

Confluence works with the wallet you already use. Connecting only shares your public wallet address with the app; it doesn't move any funds or give Confluence control of your wallet.

<Steps>

### Open the app

Go to [app.confluencebuild.xyz](https://app.confluencebuild.xyz).

### Click Connect wallet

The connect window lists the wallets it finds in your browser (for example MetaMask), plus other options such as WalletConnect for mobile wallets.

### Approve the connection in your wallet

Your wallet asks you to confirm that Confluence can see your address. Approve it, and your address appears in the header.

</Steps>

## Switching networks

When you choose a chain to send from, Confluence asks your wallet to switch to that network if it isn't already on it. If your wallet doesn't know the network yet, it may ask you to add it first. Approve the request and carry on.

<Callout type="info">
  Connecting is free and doesn't create a transaction. You only pay network fees when you actually send or swap.
</Callout>

## Disconnecting

Use the wallet menu in the header to disconnect. You can also remove Confluence from your wallet's list of connected sites at any time.
