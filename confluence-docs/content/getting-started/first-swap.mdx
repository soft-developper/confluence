# Your first swap

Swapping exchanges one token for another on the same chain, powered by Circle's App Kit.

<Steps>

### Open the Swap page

Go to **Swap** in the app header.

### Pick the chain and tokens

Choose the chain, the token you're paying with, and the token you want to receive.

### Enter the amount

Type the amount you want to swap. The app fetches a quote showing what you'll receive.

### Review and confirm

Check the rate and the minimum you'll receive, then confirm in your wallet. Depending on the token, your wallet may first ask you to approve it.

</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Prices move while you wait. The **minimum received** protects you: if the market moves past it before your swap is processed, the swap
  doesn't go through at a worse price.
</Callout>

Want a token or chain that isn't listed? Try [Relay routes](/getting-started/relay), which cover a much wider range.
