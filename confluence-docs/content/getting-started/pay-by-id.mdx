# Pay someone by @ID

If someone has a Confluence ID, you can send them USDC using their name instead of their wallet address. The money arrives in their wallet on Arc.

<Steps>

### Open Pay

Select **Pay** in the app header, then **Connect wallet** if you haven't already. If someone sent you their pay link, open it: their ID is already filled in.

### Enter their ID

In **To**, type their ID, for example `alice` or `@alice`. Confluence checks it as you type and shows the wallet it belongs to. If no one has claimed the ID, you can't continue.

### Choose where you pay from

Leave **From** on **Arc** to pay for free with a single transfer. Or pick another chain to pay from there: it's a bridge to Arc, and the bridge fee applies.

### Enter the amount

Type the amount, or use **Max**. Add a note if you like: only you and the recipient can see it.

### Review and confirm

Select **Review payment**, check the ID, wallet and amount, then confirm in your wallet. From Arc, your wallet asks once.

</Steps>

The payment shows up in your **Activity** on the Pay page, and in theirs once it's confirmed on Arc. See [Pay](/products/pay) for everything the Pay page can do.

<Callout type="info">
  Make sure you have the right ID before confirming. Like any blockchain transfer, a payment to the wrong person can't be reversed.
</Callout>
