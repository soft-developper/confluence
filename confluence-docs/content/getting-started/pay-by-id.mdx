# Pay someone by @ID

If someone has a Confluence ID, you can send them USDC using their name instead of their wallet address.

<Steps>

### Start a bridge

On the **Bridge** page, choose the chains and enter the amount, as in [your first bridge](/getting-started/first-bridge).

### Turn on Send to another address

In the recipient field, type their ID with the `@` in front, for example `@alice`.

### Check it resolves

Confluence looks up the ID and shows the wallet it belongs to. If the ID doesn't exist, you'll see **Unknown Confluence ID** and can't continue.

### Review and confirm

The review screen shows `@alice` as the recipient. Confirm in your wallet as usual.

</Steps>

The USDC arrives in their wallet on the destination chain you chose, and the payment shows up in both of your histories.

<Callout type="info">
  Make sure you have the right ID before confirming. Like any blockchain transfer, a payment to the wrong person can't be reversed.
</Callout>
