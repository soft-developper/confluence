# Using Relay

Relay routes let you bridge and swap across many more chains and tokens than CCTP covers, from the same Bridge and Swap pages. They're powered by [Relay](https://relay.link), whose network of solvers delivers your tokens on the other side.

<Steps>

### Switch to Relay

At the top of the **Bridge** or **Swap** page, switch from **Confluence** to **Relay**. Confluence is always selected when you first open the page.

### Choose what you pay and what you receive

Tap a token to open the picker: chains on one side, that chain's tokens on the other, with search. You can also paste a token's contract address.

- Pick a token on the **same chain** to **swap**.
- Pick a token on **another chain** to **bridge**.

The title changes to match, automatically.

### Enter the amount and check the quote

The quote shows what you'll receive, the minimum you'll receive, the estimated time, and a full fee breakdown: execution cost, swap cost, Relay's platform fee, and the Confluence fee.

### Review and confirm

Click **Review**, then **Confirm**. Your wallet may ask to switch networks (or add a network it doesn't know yet), approve the token, and confirm the transfer.

### Watch it complete

Each step is shown as it happens, with links to the transaction on the chain's explorer. Keep the page open until it finishes.

</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Before your wallet is ever asked to sign, Confluence checks every Relay quote against Relay's own published contracts. A quote that doesn't
  match is never shown to you.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  If Relay can't complete a route, it refunds you to the wallet you sent from, on that chain, in the token you sent, minus the gas for the refund. The refund goes to your own wallet even when you were sending to someone else. The app tells you when this happens.
</Callout>
