# Sign in and your Confluence ID

You don't need to sign in to bridge or swap. Signing in unlocks your **Profile**: your history, a synced address book, and your **Confluence ID**, the name people use to pay you.

## Sign in with your wallet

<Steps>

### Open your Profile

Click **Profile** in the app header.

### Click Sign in with wallet

Your wallet shows a message to sign. This is a standard Sign-In with Ethereum message that proves you own the address.

### Sign the message

Signing is free. It is **not** a transaction, costs no gas, and can't move your funds.

</Steps>

You stay signed in for up to 7 days on that browser.

## Claim your Confluence ID

Once signed in, you can claim a Confluence ID from your Profile.

- It's 3 to 20 characters: lowercase letters, numbers and underscores.
- It's tied to the wallet that claims it.
- Other people can use it to pay you, so it's public.

<Callout type="warning">
  **Confluence IDs are permanent.** Once claimed, an ID can't be changed or transferred to another wallet, so choose carefully.
</Callout>

## Your address book

Recipients you save are kept in your browser. When you're signed in, your address book syncs to your account, so it follows you to other devices.
