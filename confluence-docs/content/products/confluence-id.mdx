# Confluence IDs and payments

A Confluence ID is a short, readable name like `@alice` that points to your wallet. People can pay you using your ID instead of copying a 42-character address.

## Claiming an ID

[Sign in](/getting-started/sign-in) from your Profile, then claim an ID.

- 3 to 20 characters.
- Lowercase letters, numbers and underscores only.
- Tied to the wallet that claims it, and public, since others use it to pay you.

<Callout type="warning">
  **IDs are permanent.** Once claimed, an ID can't be changed, released or transferred to another wallet. Choose one you're happy to keep.
</Callout>

IDs aren't case-sensitive: `@Alice` and `@alice` are the same ID, and IDs are always shown in lowercase.

## Paying an ID

The easiest way is the [Pay](/products/pay) page: type the ID, choose where you pay from, and confirm. Payments from Arc to an ID are **free**. Payments from another chain are bridges to Arc and carry the bridge fee.

You can also pay an ID from the Bridge page: turn on **Send to another address** and type the ID with the `@`, for example `@alice`. The USDC arrives on the destination chain you choose.

Either way, Confluence looks the ID up and only lets you continue when it resolves to a wallet, and the review screen shows the ID so you can double-check it.

## Getting paid

Share your pay link or QR code from the **Receive** tab on the [Pay](/products/pay#receive) page. Payments made on the Pay page arrive in your wallet on Arc. Payments to your ID appear in your Pay **Activity** under **Received**, with the sender's ID if they have one, and bridges to your ID also appear in your Profile history.
