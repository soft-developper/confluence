# Confluence IDs and payments

A Confluence ID is a short, readable name like `@alice` that points to your wallet. People can pay you using your ID instead of copying a 42-character address.

## Claiming an ID

[Sign in](/getting-started/sign-in) from your Profile, then claim an ID.

- 3 to 20 characters.
- Lowercase letters, numbers and underscores only.
- Tied to the wallet that claims it, and public, since others use it to pay you.

<Callout type="warning">
  **IDs are permanent.** Once claimed, an ID can't be changed, released or transferred to another wallet. Choose one you're happy to keep.
</Callout>

## Paying an ID

On the Bridge page, turn on **Send to another address** and type the ID with the `@`, for example `@alice`. Confluence looks it up and only lets you continue when it resolves to a wallet. The review screen shows the ID so you can double-check it.

## Getting paid

When someone pays your ID, the USDC is minted straight to your wallet on the destination chain they chose. The payment appears in your Profile history as **Received**, with the sender's ID if they have one.
