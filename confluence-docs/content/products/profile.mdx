# Profile and history

Your **Profile** brings together your Confluence ID, your activity and your saved recipients. Open it from the app header and [sign in with your wallet](/getting-started/sign-in) to see everything.

## History

Your history lists, newest first and 20 per page:

- **Bridges** you sent, and payments you **received**.
- **Swaps**.
- **Relay routes**, marked "via Relay".

Each entry shows the amounts, the chains, when it happened and its status, such as **Complete**, **In progress**, **Needs attention**, **Refunded** or **Failed**. Click an entry to open its transaction page (for bridges) or the chain's explorer.

## Address book

Recipients you save are stored in your browser, with a label you choose. When you're signed in, your address book syncs to your account so it follows you across devices. Deleting an entry removes it everywhere.

Saved labels are also used by the lookalike check on the Bridge page: if you paste an address that resembles a saved one but isn't the same, Confluence warns you.

<Callout type="info">
  Signing in is only needed for your Profile. You can bridge, swap and use Relay routes without signing in; your transfers still have their own
  transaction pages.
</Callout>
