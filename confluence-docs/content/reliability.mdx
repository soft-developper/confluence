# Speed and reliability

## Fresh quotes

- **Bridges:** a quote is valid for 60 seconds. If it expires before you confirm, the app simply gets a new one.
- **Relay routes:** quotes refresh every 30 seconds while you look at them, and a fresh quote is fetched the moment you press Confirm.
- **While you type,** the app waits a moment after your last keystroke before asking for a quote, so you get one accurate quote instead of many.

## How long transfers take

- **CCTP bridges** depend on the chain you send from. Standard waits for that chain to finalize, which is seconds on some chains and longer on others; the app shows the expected time for each chain. **Fast** shortens it where that makes a real difference.
- **Relay routes** show an estimated time on each quote.

## When something is slow

- **Forwarding stalled?** If Circle's Forwarding Service hasn't delivered your USDC 30 minutes after attestation, the transaction page lets you complete the mint yourself. There's no risk of paying twice.
- **Attestation expired?** **Complete mint** asks Circle for a fresh attestation first, with no signature needed.
- **Relay can't complete a route?** Relay may refund you on the chain you sent from. Your history shows **Refunded**.
- **Closed the page?** No problem. Bridges are tracked in the background, and Relay routes update through Relay's signed status updates, so your history catches up on its own.

## Maintenance and pauses

If we need to pause bridging, swapping, Relay routes or a single chain, the app shows a banner explaining what's affected, and new transfers of that kind are held while the pause lasts. **Transfers already in progress keep completing**, and status tracking continues.

## Keeping Confluence running

- Confluence is monitored around the clock, including its connection to its database.
- Lists that change rarely, like Relay's chains and tokens, are cached so pages load quickly and we stay well within our partners' limits.
- Identical requests made at the same moment share one answer, which keeps things fast when many people are using the app.
- Records that never reached the blockchain (for example, a transfer you declined in your wallet) are cleaned up automatically, while anything with a transaction is always kept.

<Callout type="info">
  Your funds are on the blockchain, not on Confluence. Even if Confluence were offline, a burned CCTP transfer can still be completed with
  Circle's attestation, and Relay routes are delivered by Relay's own network.
</Callout>
