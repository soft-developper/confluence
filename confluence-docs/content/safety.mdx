# How we keep you safe

Safety starts with one design choice: **Confluence never holds your funds or your keys.** Everything else on this page builds on that.

## You sign everything

- Every approval, burn, swap and Relay transfer is a transaction **you** review and sign in your own wallet.
- Confluence has no way to move, pause or reverse your funds on its own. The only way funds move is a transaction you approve, which is also why it's worth reading what your wallet asks you to sign (see "Protect yourself" below).

## Signing in is not a transaction

Signing in uses a standard Sign-In with Ethereum message. It's free, costs no gas, and can't move funds. Your session lasts up to 7 days, and Confluence stores only a one-way fingerprint (hash) of your session, never the session itself.

## Checks before you send

- **Lookalike warning:** if a recipient looks like an address you've used or saved but is different, you're warned about address poisoning.
- **Contract warning:** if the recipient is a smart contract that may not be able to handle USDC, you're warned before you continue.
- **Gas warning:** if your wallet may not have enough gas for the transaction, you're told before your wallet fails.
- **Clear totals:** the review shows every fee and the total that leaves your wallet, before you sign.

## Verified Relay quotes

Before any Relay quote reaches your wallet, Confluence checks it against Relay's own published data for that chain:

- each transaction may only go to Relay's contracts,
- any token approval may only be granted to Relay's contracts,
- and the quote must match the tokens, chains, amount and recipient you asked for.

A quote that doesn't pass is never shown to you.

## Behind the scenes

- **Service keys never reach your browser.** Confluence's keys for Circle and Relay are kept on the server.
- **Signed updates only.** Status updates from Relay are checked for Relay's signature, so they can't be faked.
- **Rate limits** keep the service available for everyone, even under heavy or abusive traffic.
- **Protected administration.** Confluence's admin tools require two-factor authentication and are protected against repeated sign-in attempts.
- **Kill switches.** Bridging, swapping, Relay routes and individual chains can each be paused instantly if something looks wrong. Transfers already in progress keep completing.
- **Fees in a multisig.** Confluence's fees go to a 2-of-3 multisig wallet, which needs two separate keys to move anything.

## Protect yourself

<Callout type="warning">
  **Confluence will never ask for your seed phrase or private key**, and never contacts you first by direct message. Anyone who does is a
  scammer.
</Callout>

- Always check the site address is **app.confluencebuild.xyz** before connecting your wallet.
- Read what your wallet asks you to sign. Confluence's transactions go to Circle's and Relay's contracts, never to unknown addresses.
- Double-check recipient addresses character by character, especially if you copied them from your transaction history.
- Keep your wallet software up to date, and consider a hardware wallet for larger amounts.

## Report a vulnerability

If you believe you've found a security issue in Confluence, please email **support@confluencebuild.xyz** with the details and how to reproduce it. Please give us a reasonable chance to fix it before sharing it publicly. We appreciate responsible reports.
